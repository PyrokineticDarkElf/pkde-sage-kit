# Recently viewed products (kit 2026.11.07.12, TPL-134 / MGR-65)

Opt-in: `add_filter('pkde_kit/recently_viewed', '__return_true');` (a shop's `app/filters.php`). Off by default.

- **Where it shows:** a small list in the shop sidebar (`partials/sidebar-shop` prints the placeholder); a site prints it elsewhere with `{!! \App\recently_viewed_box() !!}`. Five products at a time (`pkde_kit/recently_viewed_count`, 1 to 12), a "Show more" button for the rest; up to 20 are kept (`pkde_kit/recently_viewed_max`, 1 to 50). The product being viewed is left out of its own list; products that are hidden, private, out of stock or deleted are dropped before counting, so "Show more" is honest.
- **Cache-safe:** a product page prints only an empty marker (`data-pkde-recent-view` = its own id) and the box is an empty, hidden placeholder, the same for every visitor. `resources/js/recently-viewed.js` does the rest in the browser; the products come from `?wc-ajax=pkde_recent` (GET, `no-store`, never page-cached, nothing stored).
- **Logged out:** the list is the browser's `localStorage` (`pkde_recent`: product ids only). First-party, functional, no cookie, no tracking id, nothing sent to a third party; it never leaves the browser except as the ids of the products being asked for.
- **Logged in:** the browser posts its list once per product page and where the box is (`?wc-ajax=pkde_recent_sync`, POST, same-origin only: the `Sec-Fetch-Site` header is checked because a nonce would make the page per-visitor), the server merges it into the user meta `_pkde_recently_viewed` (newest first, capped) and sends the merged ids back, so the list follows the account to a new device. The meta holds product ids and nothing else; WordPress's personal-data export and erase tools do not list it, so a privacy policy for a shop that turns this on should name it ("a list of the products you recently viewed, kept with your account").
- **Cookie policy note:** localStorage is the visitor's own browsing history held for the feature they see; a cookie banner is not needed for it under the usual "strictly necessary or functional" reading, but state it in the privacy policy.
- **Accessibility:** a labelled section, real list, real buttons, a polite live message ("N more products shown.") and focus moved to the first new product after "Show more"; no motion to reduce.
- **Look:** `--pkde-recent-line`, `--pkde-recent-muted`, `--pkde-recent-image` (3.5rem); otherwise the shop variables.
